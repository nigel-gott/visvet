You're right, it's a leftover mess from when boxes were called 'code units'. Today there are four ids for overlapping things:
- `UnitId`: a box's id (`MapBox.id`), e.g. `src/types/boxes.rs::MapBox`.
- `MemberId`: a member's id, e.g. `...::MapBox::lines`.
- `BoxId`: confusingly NOT a box's id, but either of the two above; only used for the two ends of an arrow (`Dependency`).
- `MapId`: anything you can select, the two above plus folders; used by threads and the UI hand-off.
And `UnitKind` (struct, enum, file...) plus `ParsedUnit` keep the old word.

Proposed fix, one rename, no behaviour change:
- `UnitId` → `BoxId` (a box's id, finally matching `MapBox`).
- Drop today's `BoxId`: an arrow's ends become `MapId` (anything on the map), which they already are.
- `UnitKind` → `BoxKind`, `ParsedUnit` → `ParsedBox`, and 'unit' in comments → 'box'.
- `MemberId` and `MapId` stay.
That's 13 files and contract.ts, all mechanical, and threads follow the renamed boxes now. Do it?
