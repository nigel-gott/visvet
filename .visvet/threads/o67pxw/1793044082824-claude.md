They aren't macros. #[derive(Parser)] is the only macro there. #[arg(...)] and #[command(...)] are 'helper attributes': an attribute is #[name] or #[name(...)], and the brackets hold free-form tokens that only the Parser derive reads, as settings for each field. Without the derive the compiler rejects them. So yes, they take parameters, but the derive decides what they mean. Here: #[arg(long, global = true, default_value = ".")] makes --repo a flag, allowed after any subcommand, defaulting to the current folder. #[command(subcommand)] says the cmd field is filled from the first word (tours, reply…) by choosing a Cmd variant. #[command(flatten)] pulls ServeArgs' own flags (--base, --port…) into the top-level command, so 'visvet --port 7880' works with no subcommand. The Cli step now has a note on each; reload and restart the tour to see them.
