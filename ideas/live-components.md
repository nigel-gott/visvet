# Live components

Front-end boxes that show what they draw.

1. **Near:** a picture of the component on its box, ideally made by a tool ([extracted](extracted-not-drawn.md)), e.g. a screenshot.
2. **Far:** the real component rendered on the map to play with, as a [widget](widgets.md).
