# Live components

Front-end boxes that show what they draw.

1. **Near:** a picture of the component on its box, e.g. a screenshot.
2. **Far:** the real component rendered on the map, and you can play with it.

The pictures could be [added content](added-content.md), or a [widget](widgets.md) for the live version. Ideally they'd be generated by a tool rather than drawn by the AI, as in the [data dimension](data-dimension.md).
