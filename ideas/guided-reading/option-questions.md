# Option questions

Threads gain Claude Code's question form: a series of questions, each with options to pick (and "other" for free text). The answers arrive in the thread like a reply.

- **In any thread** where the AI needs a decision: tap an option instead of typing.
- **In [tours](tours.md):** a branch point. "I built it like this; want the why, or the alternative I rejected?" Or an [understanding check](understanding-checks.md).
