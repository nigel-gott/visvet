# Understanding checks

AI first: a check doesn't have to be a [koan](koans.md).

- **A question from the AI**, answered in a mini thread inside the tour step; resolve it and the tour continues. See [option questions](option-questions.md).
- **Predict, then check:** "what does this return?" before revealing.
- **Spot the bug:** a small change to real code; find what breaks.
- **A glossary of what you've learned**, linked to where each feature appeared; feeds [fog of war](../box-signals/fog-of-war.md) with concepts you know, not just boxes seen.
- **Repetition:** concepts return in later tours until they stick.
