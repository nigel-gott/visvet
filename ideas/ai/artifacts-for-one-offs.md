# Artifacts for one-offs

Claude artifacts are web pages Claude publishes to claude.ai, private until shared. Kinds include Design (a canvas of artboards), Slides, Docs, Dashboard. They can keep a shared database, take comments, ask Claude, and save new versions of themselves.

- When a thread answer needs a diagram, a C4 picture, a statechart or a slide, the AI publishes an artifact and the thread links to it, with a preview card in the drawer.
- Docs for decision records and long explanations, edited together.
- visvet doesn't need its own diagram editor for content that stands on its own. Content that should live in the map becomes a [widget](../extending/widgets.md), since [embedding is blocked](artifact-embedding.md).
