# Overlays

What the AI adds on top of the map, beyond what is extracted from the code: positions and groupings, markers and highlights, extra arrows, text, images, any media that helps.

- [Tours](tours.md) are the main user: each step is an overlay.
- Outside a tour, overlays could still explain one area, the way a whiteboard sketch does.
- **Not from the code.** The map stays derived from the code, and an overlay is a separate layer over it that can be switched off.
- **Claude canvases:** don't rebuild them. Overlays should complement them, and maybe share a format. This deserves a long look before building.
- **Images:** e.g. a UI component's box shows what it looks like. See [live components](live-components.md).
