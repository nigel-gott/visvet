# Dimensions

What a [dimension](dimensions.md) is, then each one:

- [Data](data-dimension.md): what is stored and its shape. Where the code ends and data begins: [data models cross a boundary](data-model-boundary.md).
- [Behaviour](behaviour-dimension.md): what the system does, taken from [black-box tests](black-box-tests.md).
- [Concepts](concept-dimension.md): the ideas people explain the app with, mostly from [concept annotations](concept-annotations.md) so they don't go stale. Backlinks for agents: [visvet impact](visvet-impact.md).
- [Architecture](architecture-dimension.md): services and people, in the [C4 model](c4-model.md)'s levels, possibly [across repos](multi-repo-workspace.md).
- [Trust](trust-dimension.md), [time](time-dimension.md), [dependencies](dependencies-dimension.md): smaller ones.

[State machines](state-machines.md) aren't a dimension but a kind of picture that appears in several, made findable by [annotations](state-machine-annotations.md).

Moving between them: [switching dimensions](switching-dimensions.md), with two experiments, [stacked cards](stacked-cards.md) and [stacked planes](stacked-planes.md); and one rejected, [dimension badges](dimension-badges.md).
