# Box signals

The rule that keeps them from fighting: [one visual channel, one meaning](visual-channels.md).

Health:

- [Churn and hotspots](churn-and-hotspots.md): how often a box changes, times how complex it is.
- [Change coupling](change-coupling.md): boxes that change together with no arrow between them.
- [Dead code](dead-code.md): suspected unused boxes, from tools and reachability.
- [Countable signals](countable-signals.md): smells, slow spans, vulnerable dependencies.
- Coverage and test reach: see [tests on the map](tests-on-the-map.md).

People:

- [Ownership](ownership.md): who knows a box, from git; and knowledge at risk.
- [Pull request stripes](pr-stripes.md): which open PRs touch a box.
- [Live agents](live-agents.md): which agent is editing a box right now.

You:

- [Fog of war](fog-of-war.md): where you haven't looked or understood yet.
