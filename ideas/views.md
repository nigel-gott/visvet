# Views

A view the AI makes on top of the map, to show something: a few boxes picked and arranged, areas marked, notes and arrows drawn on them. Quick to make, e.g. in the middle of a chat or a thread ("look at these three, here's how they relate"), then thrown away or saved. (Earlier called "overlays".)

- **On top of the data, not part of it:** a view arranges and annotates; the map underneath stays as it is. Leave the view and you're back on the plain map.
- **A [tour](tours.md) is a view with steps:** walk forwards and backwards; each step picks boxes, moves the camera and adds notes. A one-off explanation is a one-step view.
- **Saved or not:** most views are temporary, part of one conversation. Some are worth keeping (a tour, an annotated overview of a section) and are committed, like threads.
- **Contents:** notes, highlights, marked areas, extra arrows, pictures and [widgets](widgets.md).
- **The difference from [added content](added-content.md):** added content is a permanent part of the map, shown to everyone always; a view is something you open.
