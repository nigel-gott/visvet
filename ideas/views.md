# Views

A view the AI makes on top of the map: a few boxes picked and arranged, areas marked, notes and arrows drawn. Quick to make, e.g. mid-chat ("look at these three, here's how they relate"), then thrown away or saved. Earlier called "overlays".

- **On top of the data:** leave the view and you're back on the plain map.
- **[Tours](tours.md) are views with steps.** A one-off explanation is a one-step view.
- **Saved or not:** most are temporary; some (a tour, an annotated overview) are committed like threads.
- **Contents:** notes, highlights, marked areas, extra arrows, pictures, [widgets](widgets.md).
- Unlike [added content](added-content.md), which is a permanent part of the map.
