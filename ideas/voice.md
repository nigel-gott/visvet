# Voice

Talk at the boxes instead of typing.

## Transcription

The Claude app's dictation can't be triggered from the page. Options:

- **Browser speech recognition:** free, built into Chrome and Android. Not in Firefox.
- **Local `whisper.cpp`:** free, fast on a Mac, works for any browser; the page sends audio to the visvet server.
- **A transcription API** (e.g. OpenAI's or Deepgram's): cheap, a fallback.

## How it feels

Clicking and typing is the core and stays as it is. Voice is a mode you turn on:

- **Click and talk:** while on, whatever you say goes to the box selected at that moment. Click another box and keep talking. No send button.
- The words appear live in the box's comment field, and are sent after a short pause unless you edit or cancel.
- **Push to talk** instead, if always-listening feels wrong: hold a key (or long-press on a phone) on a box.
