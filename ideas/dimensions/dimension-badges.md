# Dimension badges

Proposed for [switching dimensions](switching-dimensions.md): chips on the selected box ("also: concept *Thread* · data *threads/*"), a dot per linked dimension on every box, or two dimensions side by side with linked highlighting.

Dropped: boxes already carry too much, and none of these felt right. [Stacked cards](stacked-cards.md) show the same thing through the box's shape instead.
