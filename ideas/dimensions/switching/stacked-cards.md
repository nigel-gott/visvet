# Stacked cards

A box that also exists in other [dimensions](../dimensions.md) is drawn as a small stack: one or two cards peek out behind it, offset a little. No badges, no labels (maybe a thin tab edge in each dimension's colour, maybe not even that).

- Click the stack and the cards fan out, one per dimension, each showing that dimension's version (the `orders` table, the "order" concept).
- Pick one and you switch to that dimension, centred on it.
- A box with no stack exists in only one dimension, which is itself worth knowing.

The preferred first experiment for [switching dimensions](switching-dimensions.md): a box style plus a small menu, readable at a glance.
