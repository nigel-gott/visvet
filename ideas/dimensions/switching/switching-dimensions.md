# Switching dimensions

The same thing exists in several [dimensions](../dimensions.md): the concept "order", the `Order` ORM model, the `orders` table, the "place an order" behaviour. Moving between them must be easy, but boxes already carry a lot.

- **For now:** a quick way to switch dimension, keeping the selection where the other dimension has it. Then build and experiment.
- **Experiments:** [stacked cards](stacked-cards.md) (preferred first), [stacked planes](stacked-planes.md).
- **Rejected:** [dimension badges](dimension-badges.md), too much on the boxes.
